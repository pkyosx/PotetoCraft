# Attack the Premise

**Apply when** two or more fixes built on the same belief have failed the same check.

Repeated failure is evidence against the shared belief, not against the individual fixes.
Write the belief down as one sentence. It is the thing every failed fix took for granted.
Before you try another fix, test that sentence directly.
Pick a measurement whose result depends on whether the belief holds, then take it. Look at where the failures fall, not only at how big they are.
Make the check rerunnable, per [build-the-lever](build-the-lever.md).
If the evidence breaks the belief, look for what the failures share that the belief did not predict.
If the evidence holds the belief up, keep asking why, per [fix-root-causes](fix-root-causes.md). The previous attempts may have patched a symptom.
When the cause is a condition, remove the condition instead of adding something that compensates for it, per [laziness-protocol](laziness-protocol.md).

You broke this when a third fix ships on a belief nobody tested.
You followed it when the premise is written down with the observation that confirmed or refuted it, before the next fix starts.
